\section*{Baseline vs. Online Assignments}

\noindent
In Table 1 we see a simple iteration: batch mode first computes a baseline monthly plan
that allocates the initial inventory. Online mode then simulates incoming policies one
at a time with a target of $U_{\text{month}}=5$ online arrivals (total monthly inventory
$U_{\text{tot}}+U_{\text{month}}$); each arrival triggers a re-optimization
that preserves existing allocations and assigns only the incremental policy to an eligible
agency (e.g., respecting ZIP admissibility). In this table, the new policy is a high-value
(Gold) policy and its ZIP is admissible for A1, so the increment goes to A1. If the ZIP were
inadmissible for A1, the policy would be routed to the best admissible agency instead. This
process repeats until the monthly total is filled. The inventory size is $U_{\text{tot}}=10$ in batch, 11 after the first arrival
($X^{(1)}$), and 12 after the second arrival ($X^{(2)}$).

\section*{Delta (Online $-$ Batch)}

\noindent
From batch to $X^{(1)}$, A1 receives one additional Gold policy.
Row-total delta: $\Delta = [1, 0, 0, 0]^\top$.
As arrivals continue, each step adds one policy overall while row totals never
decrease; the bucket mix can adjust to stay within bounds.

\section*{What's Not in the Batch (and why)}

\noindent
Batch mode optimizes a fixed monthly inventory $U_{\text{tot}}$. Online mode then simulates
arrivals one at a time until that monthly total is reached; each arrival triggers a
re-optimization that preserves existing allocations. The following rules only matter
when adding a single new policy:

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Per-agency floors:} protect existing allocations; no agency can drop below
its previous total.
\item \textbf{ZIP lock (for the incoming policy):} if the new policy's ZIP is inadmissible
for agency $a$, that agency's total is frozen at its current count.
\item \textbf{Headroom (per-agency buffer):} allows extra slots when adding one policy at a
time to keep feasibility.
\item \textbf{Bucket bounds still apply online:} each new policy must keep Gold/Silver/Bronze
totals within their min/max limits.
\end{itemize}

\section*{Constraint Audit (Summary)}

\begin{itemize}
\item Capacity: all agencies are at capacity in batch; in online, A1 binds, others have slack.
\item Global conservation: totals are 10 (batch) and 11 (online).
\item Bucket bounds: Silver and Bronze meet their minimums; all buckets stay within bounds.
\item ZIP admissibility (online): A3 and A4 are locked at their floors.
\end{itemize}
